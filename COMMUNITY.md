# FORKLIFT Community Principles

FORKLIFT aims to be a helpful, transparent, and welcoming space for open-source collaboration.

## Our principles

1. **Build together** — share ideas, progress, and lessons learned.
2. **Be welcoming** — help newcomers and offer constructive feedback.
3. **Stay transparent** — clearly distinguish working features from proposals.
4. **Respect people** — keep discussions thoughtful and inclusive.
5. **Protect users** — never ask anyone to share a seed phrase, private key, or password.

## How to participate

Open an issue, improve documentation, share a learning resource, or review a pull request.

## Safety

Be cautious with unsolicited links, wallet connection requests, and investment claims. This repository does not provide investment advice or custody services.
